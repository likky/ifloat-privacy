The short version

iFloat does not collect your content.

Your voice, images, video, typed text, translations and history are processed by iOS on your own device. We do not ask you to create an account, we do not operate a server that receives this content, and we do not use advertising, behavioral analytics, crash-reporting or third-party tracking SDKs.

App Store privacy label: Data Not Collected. Under Apple's definition, data processed only on device and not transmitted off device is not "collected."

Note: Apple, as the provider of iOS, the App Store, StoreKit, Speech, Translation, Photos and screen capture, may process platform data under its own privacy policy. That is not our collection of that data.

2

What we collect

In short: we do not collect personal information, and we do not collect your content.

Specifically, we do not collect, read, upload or sell:

  • your name, email address, phone number, mailing address or other contact details;
  • an account, password, social account or login credential;
  • contacts, calendar, health, fitness, location or precise location;
  • advertising identifiers (IDFA), device fingerprints, unique device identifiers or cross-app tracking data;
  • your voice, recordings, photos, videos, screen content, typed text, translations or subtitles;
  • your usage behavior, tap streams, session length, search history or product analytics;
  • crash logs or performance data collected through our own SDK.

What "collect" means here

Under Apple's App Store definition, "collect" means transmitting data off the device in a way that allows the developer or a third party to access it for longer than is necessary to service the request in real time. Data processed only on your device and discarded after processing is not "collected." All core processing in iFloat falls into the latter category.

On-device data we do process

To provide the features, iFloat processes the following data on your device. It does not leave the device unless you actively export or share it:

  • Microphone audio — for live transcription and translation; raw audio is not saved by default.
  • Camera frames and photos — for text recognition and translation; photos are not saved by default, only recognized text.
  • Video/audio files you choose — for audio extraction, transcription, translation and subtitle rendering.
  • System audio and screen content — only after you start Floating or Screen Recording and complete the system picker.
  • Typed text and translations — for display, speech and local history.
  • Settings and preferences — language, appearance, voice and auto-save preferences.

iFloat does not currently request App Tracking Transparency (ATT) permission and does not include advertising or analytics SDKs.

Common questions

Do you record me?

Only when you start Live, Floating or Screen Recording and grant microphone access. Audio is transcribed on device; raw microphone audio is not saved by default. If you enable the microphone while screen recording, that audio is mixed into the video you choose to save.

Is my voice or text uploaded?

Not to our servers. Transcription, translation, text recognition and subtitle rendering happen on device. The first time you use some languages, iOS may download translation or speech resources from Apple; that process is handled by the system, and we do not receive your content.

Why does the App Store say "Data Not Collected"?

Because iFloat does not transmit your content off the device and does not operate a server that receives it. Under Apple's definition, on-device processing is not "collection."

What happens to my data if I delete the app?

History, settings and video copies inside the app container are removed when you delete the app. Copies you previously saved to Photos are not affected and must be deleted in Photos.

How do I cancel a subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple. Open iOS Settings → your Apple Account → Subscriptions, select iFloat and cancel. You can also use "Manage Subscription" on the membership screen. After cancelling, membership remains active until the end of the current paid period.

Do you sell my data?

No. We have no content data to sell, and we do not sell, rent or share personal information for advertising.

Points that are easy to misunderstand

  • Raw microphone audio is not written to disk. Live and Floating save only transcribed text. If you enable the microphone while screen recording, that audio becomes part of the video you choose to save.
  • Photos are not written to disk. Photo/library translation saves recognized text only; the app does not copy your photo into history.
  • Video copies are written to disk. Video Subtitles keeps the imported video copy, exported subtitles video and SRT in the app's private directory so you can edit again. Deleting the record deletes those copies.
  • Sharing is initiated by you. When you use the system share sheet, the file goes to the third-party app or service you choose; that third party's privacy policy applies.
  • Language resources are downloaded by the system. The first time you use some translation/speech languages, iOS may contact Apple to download resources. We do not receive your text, audio or images.

Permissions

iFloat requests permissions only when the corresponding feature needs them. You can change them at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security. Denying one permission does not affect other features.

Microphone

For Live, Floating and microphone audio during screen recording. Audio is processed on device; raw microphone audio is not saved by default.

If denied: no recording or mic audio in recordings

Speech Recognition

For turning speech into text. Recognition happens on device; some languages may require the system to download resources first.

If denied: speech transcription unavailable

Camera

For photo text capture and translation. Images are recognized and translated on device and are not uploaded.

If denied: no camera; library picker still works

Photos (add only)

For saving subtitle videos, images or screen recordings you generate to Photos. The app does not read your entire library.

If denied: cannot save to Photos; sharing still works

Screen & System Audio

Only after you start Floating or Screen Recording and choose what to share in the system picker. Nothing is captured before you confirm.

If denied: Floating/recording unavailable; other features unaffected

File & Photo Picker

Use the system file picker or PhotosPicker to choose a video/image. The picker runs outside the app; the app receives only the item you explicitly select.

No full library permission needed

Security settings

  • History index and assets use iOS file protection (completeUntilFirstUserAuthentication).
  • The history directory is excluded from iCloud backup and is not synced across devices.
  • We do not automatically delete history after a fixed number of days. Video copies can take up space; delete them when you no longer need them.

"Auto-save history" is on by default. You can turn it off in History settings; new results will no longer be saved automatically, and existing records are not deleted.

Legal rights

If you are in the EEA, UK, Switzerland, California, mainland China or another jurisdiction that grants data protection rights, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict, object, port, withdraw consent and lodge a complaint. Because we do not collect your content, many requests will result in "we do not hold your data." We will still help you delete data on your device and will respond within a reasonable period.

  • Complaints: you may contact your local data protection authority; you can also contact us first, and we will try to resolve it.

Children's privacy

iFloat is not designed for children and does not ask for age, name or an account. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Because all content is processed on device, we cannot identify a user's age or remotely view or delete content on a particular device. If a guardian believes a child has saved content they do not want to keep, they can delete history in the app or delete the app; they can also contact us for instructions.

If your jurisdiction has stricter rules for children's personal information (for example, China's rules for children under 14 or the U.S. COPPA), please use the app with a guardian and contact us if needed.

International transfers

We do not transfer your voice, images, video, text or history to other countries or regions because we do not operate a server that receives this content.

Apple's system services (such as the App Store, StoreKit, Speech, Translation, Photos and screen capture) may process platform data in various countries under their own policies. Apple is responsible for that processing, and Apple's privacy policy applies.

11

Security

iFloat reduces risk by keeping data on the device:

  • core speech recognition, translation, text recognition and subtitle rendering happen on device;
  • content is not sent to our servers, so there is no content transmission or server-storage risk on our side;
  • history index and assets use iOS file protection and are excluded from iCloud backup;
  • no advertising, analytics or third-party tracking SDKs are used.

No device is perfectly secure. We recommend setting an iPhone passcode and keeping iOS up to date. If you share a device, consider whether local history may be visible before saving sensitive content.
