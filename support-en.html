Three quick checks

  1. Allow permissions

    Open Settings → iFloat and make sure Microphone and Speech Recognition are both on. After permissions have been denied once, you need to relaunch the app for the change to take effect.

  2. Stay online while language resources download

    The first time you use a language, iOS needs to download the matching translation and dictation resources. Keep the app in the foreground, stay connected, and start once the download finishes.

  3. Check audio input and output

    Turn off silent mode and raise the volume; disconnect Bluetooth headphones and try again. While a call, recording, or another voice app holds the microphone, iFloat cannot capture audio.

If the problem only happens with one specific feature, tell us which one in your email (live translation, floating captions, screen recording or livestream, video translation, image translation) so we can narrow it down faster.

Frequently asked questions

Nothing happens after I start translating

Most often the microphone or speech recognition permission is off, or the language resources are still downloading. Work through the checks in step 1, then start translation again.

My speech isn't recognized

Make sure no other app is holding the microphone and that volume and silent mode are normal. Bluetooth headphones can take over the microphone — disconnect them and try again. Being far from the device or a noisy environment also affects recognition.

I get text, but nothing is spoken aloud

Speech output relies on system voice resources. Check the volume and silent mode; the voice for a language may need to download the first time it is used. If it is still silent, turn speech output off and on again.

Floating captions don't appear over other apps

Floating captions use the system Picture in Picture capability. Turn floating captions on in the app, confirm Settings → General → Picture in Picture → Start PiP Automatically is enabled, then switch to another app and the captions will float on screen. A few apps close Picture in Picture on their own; that is a system limitation.

Screen recordings or livestreams have no translated speech

The broadcast extension starts from Control Center: touch and hold the Screen Recording button, choose iFloat's broadcast extension, then start recording and enable the microphone option. If the extension isn't listed, add it first in Control Center → Edit Controls.

How do I cancel a subscription or request a refund?

Apple handles billing and management. To cancel: Settings → your Apple Account at the top → Subscriptions → iFloat → Cancel Subscription. You can also tap Manage Subscription on the membership screen. Refunds go through Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com. After cancelling, membership features stay available until the current paid period ends.

I changed devices and my membership didn't come back

Tap Restore Purchases on the membership screen and check that you are using the same Apple Account you purchased with. A subscription on one Apple Account works on all of your devices.

How do I delete my data?

You can delete history items one by one inside the app, and deleting the app removes everything stored in its container. Copies you saved to Photos are not affected and need to be deleted in Photos. We never upload or store your content, so there is nothing to request from us.

Which languages are supported?

Available languages come from the translation and dictation languages iOS provides, so the list changes with system versions. The language picker shows every language your device supports; if one is unavailable, download the matching language resources in the system first or update iOS.
